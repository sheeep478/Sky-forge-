❓ HOW TO PLAY

Goal: walk into every 🍲 soup store — each one stocks exactly one banana. Collect them all to win. The 🍔 cheeseburger(s) will chase you the whole time; stores are burger-proof, but they love to camp the doorway.

WASD/←↑↓→ move   SHIFT sprint (uses stamina)   P/ESC pause

Terrain: roads make you faster · walls, trees & water block you · on Spicy+ the burger drips mustard that slows you.
Power-ups: ⚡ speed boost · ❄ freezes burgers · 👻 burgers lose track of you.
Mobile: touch joystick appears automatically. Frisk always starts on the left side of the map; the burger lurks on the right.

SKYFORGE

Build · Survive · Explore · Forge a Portal

How to play

Move with WASD or the left thumbstick · look with the mouse or by dragging.

Mine by holding break — harder blocks take longer; the right tool is faster. Place blocks to build.

Inventory & crafting: press E (or tap 🎒). Equip armor by tapping it; craft logs → planks → sticks → tools.

🌾 Farming: craft a hoe, use it on grass/dirt to till, plant wheat seeds (from grass), wait for them to grow gold, then harvest → bread. Eat food (use it) to refill hunger.

Night is dark and dangerous — zombies spawn after dusk. Craft torches (coal + stick) for light, and a sword to fight back. Attack with the break action.

Logs face the way you place them. Creative: double-tap jump to fly.

🔥 Nether portal: build a 4×5 obsidian frame, then light it (press G / 🔥) and step through.

SKYFORGE

Build · Survive · Explore · Forge a Portal

How to play

Move with WASD or the left thumbstick · look with the mouse or by dragging.

Mine by holding break — harder blocks take longer; the right tool is faster. Place blocks to build.

Inventory & crafting: press E (or tap 🎒). The inventory has a 2×2 grid; a crafting table opens the full 3×3. Tap an item, then a grid cell, then the result.

🪣 Buckets (3 iron) scoop & pour water/lava — put lava + water a block apart for a cobblestone generator. 🐑 Sheep drop wool for beds.

🔴 Redstone: dust, blocks, levers, buttons, torches (NOT gates), lamps, repeaters, comparators (use to toggle subtract) and pistons — enough to build logic gates, adders and full calculators. Flip levers / press buttons / set comparators with the place action.

🌾 Farming: craft a hoe, till grass/dirt (it's wet & faster near water), plant seeds from tall grass, let them grow gold, harvest → bread. Eat food to refill hunger.

🛏️ Beds: craft a bed, sleep at night to skip to morning and set your respawn point. 🌳 Plant saplings to grow trees.

Night is dark and dangerous — zombies, skeletons (they shoot arrows), spiders and creepers (they hiss and explode!) spawn after dusk. Craft torches (coal + stick) for light, a sword to fight, and a bow (string + sticks) with arrows (flint + stick + feather) for ranged. Sprint with Ctrl / R (or push the stick to the edge).

🍖 Food: hunt cows (raw beef + leather), pigs and chickens, then cook the meat in a furnace for far more hunger. Apples drop from leaves; ring one with gold for a Golden Apple.

🧱 Building blocks: craft slabs (3 in a row), stairs (stair pattern), fences (planks + sticks) and glass panes (6 glass). Slabs go on the half you click; stairs face the way you're looking; fences and panes auto-connect to their neighbours.

🚪 Doors, trapdoors & ladders: craft doors (6 planks) and trapdoors (6 planks) — tap them to open/close. Place ladders (sticks) on a wall and hold forward or jump to climb.

Logs face the way you place them. Creative: double-tap jump to fly.

🔥 Nether portal: build a 4×5 obsidian frame, then light it (press G / 🔥) and step through. Hunt blazes in nether-brick fortresses for blaze rods.

👁️ To the End: craft Eyes of Ender (ender pearl + blaze powder). Hold one (press T) to get a bearing to the nearest stronghold; dig down to its End-portal room and set an eye into each of the 12 frames to open the portal.

🐉 Ender Dragon: in the End, smash every glowing End Crystal on the pillars (they heal the dragon), then strike the dragon when it perches at the centre. Beat it to claim the Dragon Egg and open the way home.

⚙️ Create — kinetics: craft Andesite Alloy (stone + iron) → shafts & cogwheels. Power them with a Water Wheel (place beside water), a Hand Crank (tap to spin), or the Creative Motor (always-on, creative only). Shafts pass rotation straight through; touching cogwheels mesh and spin opposite ways. A big and a little cogwheel won't turn together — match sizes (small↔small, large↔large). Placement assist: a blue holographic part shows where it'll snap — look down a shaft to extend it in a line, and new parts align to what you place them against. The held item's name shows above the hotbar.

🛠️ Create — machines: tap a powered Millstone (grinds ore→crushed, wheat→flour, cobble→gravel→sand), Mechanical Press (ingots→sheets) or Encased Fan (with lava in front it smelts, with water it washes crushed ore into ingots) to load an item; tap again empty-handed to collect the result.
